Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Insync International SRL handles information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

When you send an enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, project location, project type, approximate project size and message.

How We Use Information

We use enquiry information to review your project request, contact you about your enquiry and provide relevant construction information or quotations.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share enquiry details with relevant project professionals or service providers only when needed to respond to your request or operate the website.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, project assessment, legal, accounting or business purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@insyncbuilders.com.
